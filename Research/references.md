## Evidence note

The Amazon Reviews'23 statistics quoted in the README and source comparison are taken from the published dataset documentation. They describe the source dataset, not an analysis performed in this project. The `All_Beauty` category is broader than skincare. Any skincare-specific subset and its statistics must be established by inspecting the product metadata after access.

The source pages should be revisited before use to verify current availability, attribution instructions, and permitted terms of use. A public webpage or downloadable dataset is not automatically free of reuse or redistribution restrictions.
